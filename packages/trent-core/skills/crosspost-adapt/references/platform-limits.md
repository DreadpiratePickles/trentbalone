# Platform limits and manners, as of September 2026

Numbers a seat should not carry in its head. When a platform changes a limit, change it here.

| Platform | Text limit | Links | Hashtags | Media notes | Manners |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| X | 280 characters per post; threads for more | One, at the end or in a reply; links count against the limit | None inside sentences; one or two at the end at most | Images and video attach; alt text available | Hook first; the first post of a thread stands alone; pay-per-use API pricing for automation |
| LinkedIn | About 3,000 characters; the first two lines show before "see more" | External links in the body are shown less; put the link in the first comment | Up to three, at the end | Documents (carousels), images, video | First person on a profile; one idea per line; no "click the link below" |
| Instagram | 2,200-character caption; the first line shows in feed | Not clickable in captions; "link in bio" or a booking phrase | Up to five at the end (30 is the technical limit; it reads as spam) | Publishing through the API needs a hosted image URL; carousels up to ten; AI-generated content has a disclosure field | Hook line, blank line, short lines, alt text every time |
| Threads | 500 characters | Clickable | One topic tag | Images, video, carousels of 2 to 20 | Conversational; questions get replies |
| Bluesky | 300 characters | Shown as a card | Work as text; no ranking effect | Images with alt text; 50 MB blobs | No algorithmic feed to game; write to be replied to |
| Facebook Page | Long posts are fine | Inline, shown as a card | At most two | Images, video, scheduled 10 minutes to 30 days out | End with a question or an invitation |
| TikTok | Caption up to 4,000 characters; short reads better | Not clickable in captions | Three to five | Publishing through the API is private-only until the app is audited | On-screen text matters more than the caption |
| YouTube Shorts | Title under 60 characters; description up to 5,000 | Clickable in the description | In the description | Uploads through the API are private until the project is audited; the AI-use disclosure applies to altered content | The title is the hook |
| Google Business Profile | 1,500 characters; 150 to 300 reads best | A button (Book, Call, Learn more) | None | One image | Plain facts; read from search results by people about to phone |

## Common rules across all of them

- One link per post, one ask per post.
- Facts (price, date, claim) never change between versions.
- Alt text on every image, everywhere it is supported.
- Say "AI-generated image" in the caption and the alt text if one is used.
