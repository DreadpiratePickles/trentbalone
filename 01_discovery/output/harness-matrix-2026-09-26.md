# Harness matrix, 2026-09-26

One matrix comparing what the leading agent harnesses ship with what Trent has at HEAD `412f5b0`
(`feature/trent-fleet-v2`), then (a) the twenty gaps a small-business, social-media or creator user
would notice first, (b) where Trent is ahead of every other column, and (c) a local-models sub-table
built from the local-path audit's gaps G1-G18. Read-only synthesis for
`docs/sessions/2026-09-26-harness-landscape.md`; no web research, no tests run, no subagents.
