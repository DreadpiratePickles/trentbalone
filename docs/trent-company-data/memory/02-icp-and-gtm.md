## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Primary ICP — Solo technical founder

- **Who:** 1-person or 2-person technical startup, pre-Series A
- **Context:** Building a SaaS or dev tool, has product-market fit signal, but stuck
  managing operations that eat 30-40% of their week
- **Pain:** Can't hire fast enough; tasks pile up in Notion docs and Slack threads
  that nobody executes on; spending $20k/mo on contractors for work agents could do
- **Budget:** $200–$1,000/mo for productivity software
- **Watering holes:** Hacker News, X (tech Twitter), Linear, GitHub, Product Hunt
- **Decision trigger:** Saw a competitor automate something they do manually

### Secondary ICP — Ops lead at seed-stage startup (5–20 people)

- **Who:** Head of Ops or founding engineer forced into ops at a 10-person company
- **Context:** Company growing faster than process; CEO's Slack DMs are the backlog
- **Pain:** Every week something important falls through the cracks; no system of
  record for what the agents/contractors are actually doing
- **Budget:** $500–$2,000/mo
- **Watering holes:** Lenny's Newsletter, SaaStr, First Round Review

## Anti-ICP (do not sell to)

- Enterprise (>50 people) — compliance and procurement cycle kill momentum
- Non-technical founders — they cannot debug when agents make mistakes
- Agencies — their revenue model depends on human labor; Trent threatens them
