- Design decisions (recorded before GREEN):
  - The verdict rides the terminal frame as `event.verdict` and the snapshot as `snapshot.verdict`
    (`verdict.ts` `verdictOf` reads either). `types.ts` mirrors the app and is not grown.
  - reason: `model_calls_failed` (a step's model calls failed, the planner's or the consolidator's
    call failed) | `run_error` (the drain died on something else). No verdict -> the frame is left
    as the pipeline made it.
  - The real cause: a wrapper around the app's seat executor, inside the run meter, watches every
    port attempt and keeps the first real provider failure (never a "not configured" refusal); it
    replaces `result.error`, so the tally, the step frames and auto-recovery all see google 429
    instead of the last refusal, with its Retry-After.
  - Recovery window = what one re-run can wait inside the gateway: `DEFAULT_RETRY_POLICY.capMs x
    (attempts - 1)` = 16 s. A 429 asking for longer is not re-run; the step fails at once naming
    the wait, and `retryAfterSeconds` is on the step and the verdict for the caller.
  - Consolidation: `completed` | `failed` (the wrapper consolidator's call threw) | `skipped` (the
    run never consolidated, or a step already failed on rate_limit/auth and the wrapper does not
    ask that provider again).
  - Exit codes: `trent run` exits 5 (EXIT.PROVIDER) when the verdict's reason is
    `model_calls_failed`, 1 for any other failure; getting-started's table gains the row.
