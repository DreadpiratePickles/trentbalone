# AI Cofounder Operating System

Summary: An AI cofounder operating system is a multi-agent SaaS product that turns a founder's goal into recurring autonomous work across planning, product development, marketing, customer operations, and business analytics, while preserving user control through permissions, budgets, approvals, and audit logs.

## Definition

An AI cofounder operating system differs from a chatbot or coding assistant because it has persistent company state, role-based agents, scheduled operating cycles, tool integrations, and measurable business outputs. The system does not merely answer questions; it plans work, executes tasks, records outcomes, and learns from company activity.

## Required capabilities

- Company memory: vision, goals, customer profile, positioning, product state, credentials, documents, metrics, and decisions.
- Agent organization: CEO/planner, engineer, growth marketer, content marketer, support operator, analyst, and finance/cost monitor.
- Operating cycles: scheduled runs that inspect state, prioritize tasks, execute work, and summarize results.
- Tool gateway: scoped access to code repositories, email, ad platforms, social accounts, payments, hosting, databases, browser sessions, and analytics.
- Human control: permissions, approvals, budget limits, kill switches, pause/unpause, public visibility toggles, and audit logs.
- Business surfaces: dashboards, tasks, reports, documents, cost tracking, live activity, and public company pages.

## Design principle

The category only becomes credible when it connects strategy to execution. A product that writes code but cannot validate demand, launch acquisition, handle customers, or measure outcomes is an AI developer, not an AI cofounder.
