## Category we're creating

We are not trying to win in "AI assistant" or "project management."
We are creating a new category: **AI company operating system.**

The frame: every startup has a set of operating functions (engineering, growth,
content, support, finance). Today those require humans or a mess of disconnected
tools. Trent makes those functions run on AI agents with a single interface and a
shared operating loop.

---

## The positioning statement

**For** solo technical founders and early-stage startup operators
**Who** are drowning in operational tasks that eat time better spent on product
**Trent is** an AI company operating system
**That** runs your daily operations — engineering, growth, content, support, finance
  — through autonomous AI agents you can direct with a single command
**Unlike** AI assistants (Notion AI, ChatGPT) that only help you write,
  or project managers (Linear, Jira) that only track work,
**Trent** actually executes the work.

## Messages by audience

### For solo founders on HN / X

> "I built the boring parts of my company with AI and stopped being my own ops hire.
> Trent runs my support queue, writes my changelogs, manages my ad campaigns, and
> files a morning briefing every day. I just approve or redirect."

### For growth marketers evaluating tools

> "Your growth agent doesn't just suggest — it sets up the A/B test, writes the
> copy variants, launches the campaign, and reports results. You approve the spend."

### For engineers curious about AI tooling

> "Workbench is a build session where an AI agent plans, writes, tests, and deploys
> your code. You get a live preview and approve before it merges. It's not Copilot
> — it runs the whole task."

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I don't trust AI to run my company" | "You set approval rules. Agents pause and ask on anything that matters. Nothing leaves without your sign-off." |
| "It's too expensive" | "One agent saves 4–8 hours/week. At $49/mo that's less than an hour of contractor time." |
| "I tried AI tools before and they don't follow through" | "Trent has an operating cycle — it checks back on every open task every day. Things don't fall through the cracks." |
| "What if the AI makes a mistake?" | "Every action is in the audit log. You can see exactly what it did and why. You can reverse most things." |
