# Public Launch Checklist

Related: [[positioning]] [[roadmap]]

Target launch: **Product Hunt — first Tuesday in July 2026**

---

## Pre-launch (4 weeks out)

- [ ] Workbench GA — feature complete and stable
- [ ] Landing page updated with Workbench demos
- [ ] Demo video recorded (< 90 seconds, shows a full cycle)
- [ ] Pricing page live with all 4 tiers
- [ ] 10 beta users onboarded + at least 5 written testimonials collected
- [ ] "Built with Trent" section on landing page (Trent running itself as proof)
- [ ] Changelog for past 3 months written and published
- [ ] Support doc: "Getting started in 10 minutes"
- [ ] Email sequence: welcome (day 0), first cycle (day 2), upgrade nudge (day 7)

## Launch week prep

- [ ] PH submission drafted: headline, tagline, gallery images, first comment
- [ ] HN Show HN post drafted (link to demo, personal founder story)
- [ ] X thread drafted: 10-tweet thread with GIFs
- [ ] Reach out to 20 supporters for upvotes on launch day
- [ ] Slack community invite link ready to share
- [ ] Grafana dashboard open for live traffic monitoring

## Launch day

- [ ] PH goes live at 12:01 AM PT
- [ ] Post HN Show HN at 9 AM ET
- [ ] Post X thread at 8 AM ET
- [ ] Respond to every PH comment within 2 hours
- [ ] Monitor Sentry for any new errors under traffic
- [ ] Have rollback plan ready (feature flag to disable Workbench if unstable)

## Post-launch (48 hours)

- [ ] Email list: send to waitlist with "we launched" message
- [ ] Analyze signups by source (PH vs HN vs X)
- [ ] Write and publish a "what we learned" post for the blog
- [ ] Thank supporters personally
- [ ] Update roadmap with feedback themes from comments
