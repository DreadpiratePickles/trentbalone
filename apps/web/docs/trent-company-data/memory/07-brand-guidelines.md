# Trent — Brand Guidelines

**Upload to Memory so Content and Growth agents write on-brand copy.**

---

## Voice & tone

**What we are:**
- Direct and confident, not arrogant
- Technical-enough to earn respect from engineers
- Warm enough that solo founders feel less alone
- Excited about what's possible — not hype-y about what we can't do yet

**What we are not:**
- Corporate or stuffy ("leverage synergies," "best-in-class")
- Over-hyped AI marketing speak ("revolutionary," "game-changing," "10x")
- Cutesy or emoji-heavy
- Verbose — we cut every sentence that doesn't earn its place

## Writing rules

1. Lead with what the user gets, not what Trent does
2. Use active voice: "Trent runs your stand-up" not "stand-ups are run by Trent"
3. Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
4. Never use: leverage, synergy, empower, unlock, revolutionize, game-change
5. Numbers beat adjectives: "saves 4 hours/week" beats "saves significant time"
6. Acknowledge limits honestly — we're early, we break sometimes, we're improving

## Sample copy — approved

**Hero headline options:**
- "Your company, on autopilot."
- "The AI ops layer your startup actually needs."
- "Run your company. Trent handles operations."
- "One interface. Seven agents. Zero dropped balls."

**Feature callouts:**
- "Trent writes the code, runs the tests, and waits for your green light."
- "Every night, Trent reviews everything that's open and moves it forward."
- "Your agents work while you sleep. Approvals wait in your inbox."

**Error message tone:**
- Not: "An unexpected error has occurred."
- Yes: "That didn't work — here's what Trent tried and where it got stuck."

## Visual identity

- Primary color: deep navy (#080a0f background, #0e131d panels)
- Accent: electric blue (#60a5fa links, active states)
- Warm amber: (#C0915A) for Claude model indicator
- Text: cool off-white (#e7edf6 primary, #cbd7e7 body, #92a3b8 muted)
- Font: System UI stack — no custom font (fast, readable, professional)
- Icons: Lucide icons, 16px / 20px, stroke-based
- Border radius: minimal (2–4px) — workmanlike, not rounded-bubble
- Motion: subtle, functional — no decorative animation

## Logo usage

- Mark: "Trent" in text, no icon required
- On dark: white text
- On light: #080a0f text
- Minimum size: 40px height
- Clear space: 1× mark height on all sides

## Product naming

- The product: **Trent**
- The AI agents: "your agents" or "Trent agents" (not "bots" or "AI bots")
- The operating cycle: "cycle" (not "workflow" or "sprint")
- The knowledge base: "Vault" (not "Wiki" in external copy)
- The build surface: "Workbench" (not "IDE" or "code editor")
- The founder's chat: "Command" (not "chat" or "assistant")
