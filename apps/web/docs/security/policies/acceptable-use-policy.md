# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective:** 2026-05-28  
**Owner:** CEO / founding team  
**Review cadence:** Annually

---

## 1. Purpose

Define the acceptable use of Trent systems, production access, and customer data by all team members and contractors.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees, contractors, and advisors with access to Trent systems, code repositories, or customer data.

## 3. Acceptable use

Team members may:
- Access production systems for legitimate business purposes (debugging, deployments, customer support)
- Use company AI tools (OpenAI, Anthropic) for Trent product development
- Share non-confidential technical information publicly (blog posts, talks) with CEO approval

## 4. Prohibited use

Team members must NOT:
- Access customer data for any purpose other than providing the service or resolving a support issue
- Share customer data with unauthorized parties or use it to train external models
- Store credentials, API keys, or secrets in code, Slack, email, or any unencrypted location
- Disable or bypass security controls (rate limiting, RBAC, audit logging)
- Use personal devices for production access without enabling full-disk encryption and screen lock
- Share production access credentials with anyone not on the approved access list

## 5. Device security

All devices used to access Trent production systems must have:
- Full-disk encryption enabled (FileVault on macOS, BitLocker on Windows)
- Screen lock set to ≤ 5 minutes idle
- Up-to-date OS and browser patches
- No unauthorized software that could compromise the device

## 6. Reporting violations

Any observed or suspected violation of this policy must be reported to the CEO or security contact immediately. Good-faith reporting is protected — no retaliation for reporting a violation.

## 7. Acknowledgment

All team members must acknowledge this policy in writing (email confirmation to CEO) before receiving production access.
